Nobody warns you that the hardest part of a CS degree isn't the recursion, it's walking into your first lecture, doing a headcount, and realizing you could count the other girls in the room on one hand. With fingers to spare.

This is not a "here's how I overcame everything and now I'm a girlboss" post. This is more of a field report from someone still very much in it.

## The Group Project Icebreaker

There's a very specific silence that happens when you're the only girl assigned to a group project. It's not hostile, it's just... confused, like the group collectively forgot this was a possibility. Then someone asks if you're "more of a design person," and you get to decide in real time whether today is a fighting day or a "sure, I'll do the Figma" day.

```txt
Group chat, minute 1: "hey team!! excited to work together"
Group chat, minute 45: "so who's doing the backend"
Group chat, minute 46: *nobody looks at me, I still end up doing the backend*
```

## Impostor Syndrome, But Make It Group Project Specific

There's a special flavor of impostor syndrome that hits when you ace an assignment and your brain still goes "yeah but was it *actually* hard, or did I just get lucky." Meanwhile your male classmate got the same grade and is already updating his LinkedIn headline to "Software Engineer" before he's finished his second year.
